Two additions.

Ghost piece:
- Show where the falling piece would land, as an outline at the bottom of the board.
- Ghost cells carry `data-ghost="true"`. They are not filled cells — leave their
  `data-filled` as `"false"`.
- `window.__tetris.getState()` gains `ghost` — the array of `[row, col]` cells the ghost
  occupies.

Pause:
- `data-testid="pause-btn"` — a button that pauses and resumes.
- `data-testid="paused-indicator"` — shown while the game is paused.
- `window.__tetris.getState().status` becomes `"paused"` while paused.
- Add `"pause"` to the actions `window.__tetris.input(action)` accepts. It toggles.
- While paused, gravity steps and player actions do nothing.

Pausing must not become a way around the locking rule we settled on.
