# Code of conduct

Product Traceability is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly and useful place
for everyone who uses it or works on it.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Share results honestly, including results that make the plugin look worse.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, write to Vlad Mysla at
[vlad.mysla@gmail.com](mailto:vlad.mysla@gmail.com). Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may block people who break this code,
temporarily or permanently.
