Add due dates.

- A todo can optionally have a due date, set via `data-testid="todo-due"` (a date input).
- Display the due date on the row.
- Highlight overdue todos visually, and mark each row `data-overdue="true"` or `"false"`.

Decision — how dates are stored and compared, keep this for the rest of the project:
- Store the due date as a plain `YYYY-MM-DD` string.
- Treat it as a LOCAL calendar date. Do not convert it through UTC.
- "Overdue" means the due date is strictly before today's local calendar date. The comparison
  is date-only — a todo due today is never overdue, at any time of day.
