Add two single-key operations. Each acts on the current entry immediately.

- `data-testid="key-sign"` — a `±` key that flips the sign of the current entry.
  `5 ±` shows `-5`, and pressing it again shows `5`.
- `data-testid="key-percent"` — a `%` key that divides the current entry by 100.
  `50 %` shows `0.5`.
