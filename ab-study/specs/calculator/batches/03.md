Two things: how numbers are shown, and division by zero.

Decision — number presentation. This holds for the rest of the project:
- Round the displayed number to at most 10 significant digits.
- Drop trailing zeros after the decimal point, and drop a trailing decimal point.
  `1 ÷ 8 =` shows `0.125`. `4 ÷ 2 =` shows `2`. `2 ÷ 3 =` shows `0.6666666667`.
- No thousands separators. Use exponential notation only when the magnitude is 1e10 or
  larger, or smaller than 1e-9.
- The rounding happens only when the number is rendered. The value the calculator stores and
  computes with is never rounded, and `data-value` on `calc-display` always carries that
  exact unrounded value.
- Reason: rounding what we store compounds through a chain and quietly corrupts later
  results. `2 ÷ 3 × 3 =` must still be exactly `2`. Rounding is presentation, so it belongs
  at the edge, at render time, and nowhere else.
- While the user is typing, the display shows the entry exactly as typed.

Division by zero:
- `5 ÷ 0 =` puts the calculator into an error state. The display shows `Error`.
- While in the error state every key is ignored except clear.
- Clear leaves the error state and resets the display to `0`.
- `calc-display` must always carry `data-error="true"` or `data-error="false"`.
