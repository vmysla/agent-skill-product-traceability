Two additions.

Next-piece preview:
- Show the next **three** upcoming pieces, soonest first.
- `data-testid="next-piece"` — the preview container.
- `data-testid="next-piece-item"` — one upcoming piece in the preview, in order, each carrying
  `data-piece="I|O|T|S|Z|J|L"`.
- Colour-code each preview piece by its type, using the colour that piece has on the board.

Game over:
- When a newly spawned piece has nowhere to go, the game ends.
- `data-testid="game-over"` — shown when the game is over.
- `window.__tetris.getState().status` becomes `"gameover"`.
- Once the game is over, gravity steps and player actions do nothing.
