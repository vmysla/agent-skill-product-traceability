Two additions.

Levels and speed:
- The level starts at 1 and goes up by one for every 10 lines cleared. It never goes down.
- The higher the level, the faster gravity runs. The interval between gravity steps, in
  milliseconds, is `max(100, 1000 - (level - 1) * 100)`.
- `window.__tetris.getState()` gains `dropInterval` — that interval in milliseconds.
- `window.__tetris.tick(n)` is unchanged: it always advances exactly n gravity steps, whatever
  the level is.
- Add `window.__tetris.setLevel(n)` — sets the current level to n, for testing. Levelling from
  lines carries on from there, and the level still never goes down.

Hold:
- One hold slot. Holding puts the falling piece aside and brings in the next one. Holding again
  swaps the falling piece with the one being held. A piece coming out of hold enters at the
  spawn position.
- Only one hold per piece: after holding, a second hold is refused until a piece locks.
- Add `"hold"` to the actions `window.__tetris.input(action)` accepts.
- `window.__tetris.getState()` gains `hold` — the letter of the held piece, or `null`.
- `data-testid="hold-piece"` — the hold display, carrying `data-piece` with the held letter.
