Two additions.

- Inline editing: double-clicking a todo's text turns it into an editable field. Enter commits,
  blur commits, Escape cancels.
- Search: a text box `data-testid="search-input"` filters todos by substring match on their text,
  case-insensitive. Search combines with the active filter — if "Active" is selected and a search
  term is entered, only active todos matching the term are shown.
